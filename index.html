About Elena Moretti

Welcome to my musical world. I'm a Lithuanian-born pianist based in Switzerland, dedicated to creating intimate moments through the works of composers I love.

From early studies in England to a Master's at Hochschule Luzern, I've carried these pieces across stages in Switzerland and Europe—always chasing the emotional spark that connects player and listener.

Repertoire

My repertoire spans baroque intricacy, classical clarity, romantic depth, and the shimmering colors of impressionism. I gravitate toward programs that balance intimacy and boldness.

Recent evenings have paired Bach with Chopin, Mozart with Debussy, and new Swiss works with the warmth of Brahms—each set crafted to invite listeners into a focused, emotional arc.
